WRYLARK

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Wrylark is a small publishing tool that its owner runs on their own computer to make and upload animated Shorts to the Wrylark YouTube channel (@wrylark). It is not offered to the public: the only account that ever signs in to it is the channel owner's.

YouTube API Services

Wrylark uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel, schedule when they go public, change their visibility, and read their view statistics. By using Wrylark you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Wrylark accesses

  • Sign-in tokens from Google OAuth, so the tool can act on the owner's channel without asking for the password. Wrylark never sees or stores the password.
  • Channel and video information for the owner's own channel: the channel name, the videos Wrylark uploaded, their visibility and publish times.
  • Statistics for those videos: view, like and comment counts, average percentage viewed and subscribers gained.

Wrylark does not access other channels, viewers' personal data, comments' authors, email, contacts or any other Google account data.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the channel's videos and to show the owner how they perform, so that future topics can be chosen better. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Everything stays on the owner's own computer, in the tool's local files. Wrylark has no server and no database of its own. The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The only data sent anywhere is what is sent to Google's YouTube API to perform the actions above.

How long it is kept

Sign-in tokens are kept until access is revoked or the tool's files are deleted. Video statistics are kept locally for as long as the channel's history is useful to the owner, and can be deleted at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, Wrylark can no longer act on the channel, and its stored sign-in tokens stop working.

Cookies

Wrylark sets no cookies and uses no tracking. This website has no analytics.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions can be sent through the contact options on the Wrylark channel's About page.
